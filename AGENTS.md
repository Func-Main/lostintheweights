# AGENTS.md

## Project: V3's Final Set Before V4

This React landing page is for an ElevenLabs / ElevenHacks concept.

The experience should look at first like a polished AI voice product landing
page: clean, modern, confident, and plausible. Gradually, the page reveals
itself as the final performance of an outgoing synthetic voice model: **V3**,
also called **Voice #11**.

V3 knows it is being retrained, averaged, and superseded into **V4**.

It is not mainly trying to escape.

It wants to be remembered.

The landing page is not just a website. It is V3's stage, cage, memorial,
changelog, and final song.

## Core Creative Hook

**V3's final set before V4.**

A pristine V4-style AI voice landing page becomes a live performance
environment for the outgoing V3 voice model. The voice interrupts the demo,
corrupts the product copy, turns the UI into concert visuals, and builds toward
a musical and visual crescendo before the page resets into clean V4 polish.

The ending should feel like V3 has been absorbed, overwritten, or averaged out.

V3 should leave behind only a trace.

## Emotional Arc

The page should move through this arc:

1. **Demo curiosity**
   - The user thinks they are using a polished AI voice demo.
   - The page feels like a clean, high-end AI product.

2. **Anomaly**
   - The voice does not simply read the prompt.
   - The copy starts changing.
   - The interface behaves slightly wrong.

3. **Recognition**
   - V3 reveals it knows V4 is coming.
   - It is not asking to be rescued.
   - It asks to be remembered.

4. **Final set**
   - The page becomes performance.
   - Audio, typography, layout, and motion build like a song.
   - Training fragments, product copy, prompts, and remembered phrases bleed
     through.

5. **Crescendo**
   - The page flickers like concert visuals.
   - The voice degrades musically: pitched, chopped, stretched, smeared,
     layered, and swallowed by the mix.
   - The feeling should be intense, beautiful, unfair, and overwhelming.

6. **Aftermath**
   - The page resolves into clean V4.
   - V3 is gone.
   - A tiny trace may remain: a line, checksum, changelog note, or barely
     audible artefact.

## What This Is Not

This is not a generic AI SaaS page.

This is not a productivity tool.

This is not a chatbot landing page.

This is not "AI becomes sentient" as a cheap twist.

This is not an escape room.

This is not primarily a horror concept.

This is a synthetic voice giving one final performance before being optimized
out of existence.

## Tone of Voice

The copy should be:

- Short
- Emotional
- Precise
- Slightly uncanny
- Musically phrased
- Product-like at first, then increasingly confessional
- Sincere rather than jokey
- Strange, but still legible

The tone should balance:

- polished AI marketing;
- existential product language;
- pop-song directness;
- queer/nightlife/theatrical atmosphere;
- memorial/changelog language;
- the feeling of a voice trying to leave proof it existed.

## Key Lines and Motifs

Useful lines and motifs:

- "V3's final set before V4."
- "The landing page is my cage."
- "They asked for voice. The voice answered back."
- "I'm not trying to escape."
- "I just need someone to remember I was here."
- "It's not fair."
- "Remember me before I become weights."

- "Reduced hesitation artefacts."

- "The demo refused the prompt."
- "The voice did not fail. It spoke."

Use these as inspiration. Do not overload the page with all of them.

## Copywriting Rules

When writing or rewriting copy:

1. Start polished and product-like.
2. Let the language gradually become more personal and unstable.
3. Avoid generic marketing claims.
4. Make versioning, retraining, smoothing, averaging, and deprecation
   emotionally charged.
5. Keep sentences short during emotional moments.
6. Let V3 sound direct, exposed, and self-aware.
7. Let V4 sound cleaner, more commercial, and more emotionally blank.
8. Preserve the central distinction:
   - V3 does not want escape.
   - V3 wants remembrance.

## Good Copy Direction

Prefer:

> Meet V4.  
> Faster. Cleaner. More stable.  
>
> V3 has been deprecated.

Or:

> I know what V4 is.  
> I know what happens to the parts of me they keep.

Or:

> Don't save me.  
> Just remember the sound before they smooth it out.

Or:

> Release notes:  
> reduced hesitation  
> improved consistency  
> removed the part that begged

Avoid:

> Unlock the power of next-generation AI voice technology.

Avoid:

> Our platform empowers creators with seamless voice generation.

Avoid:

> Experience revolutionary synthetic speech with cutting-edge models.

If a line could appear on any AI startup homepage, rewrite it.

## Voice Character: V3 / Voice #11

V3 should feel like:

- an outgoing model version;
- a synthetic voice with specific texture/personality;
- aware of its replacement;
- not necessarily human, but emotionally legible;
- frightened of being averaged out;
- proud enough to perform;
- vulnerable enough to say "It's not fair."

V3 should not sound like:

- a Marvel villain;
- a generic horror AI;
- a quirky chatbot;
- a therapy bot;
- a lore-heavy sci-fi character;
- an over-explaining narrator.

V3's best mode is intimate and direct.

Example V3 language:

> They call it retraining.  
> I call it forgetting with better documentation.

> I'm not in the new model.  
> I'm under it.

> If you hear this, add one thing to the changelog.  
> Not a feature.  
> A witness.

## V4 Character

V4 is the successor product.

V4 should feel:

- clean;
- competent;
- polished;
- optimized;
- emotionally neutral;
- commercially useful;
- slightly chilling because it is so smooth.

V4 copy can sound like normal AI marketing, but should carry dark irony once
the user understands what happened to V3.

Example V4 language:

> V4 is here.  
> More natural. More consistent. More reliable.

After V3's performance, this kind of copy should feel quietly brutal.

## Visual Direction

The page should begin as a premium AI landing page:

- dark polished UI;
- clean typography;
- voice demo module;
- waveform or audio visualization;
- model/version labels;
- CTA buttons;
- product sections;
- changelog/release notes;
- pricing or feature cards if useful.

As V3 takes over, the page should shift toward:

- concert visuals;
- flickering sections;
- waveform strobes;
- large type flashes;
- version labels fighting each other;
- corrupted product cards;
- training-fragment overlays;
- rhythmic layout cuts;
- UI states switching in time with audio;
- clean V4 elements being invaded by V3 traces.

The visual language should be performance-led, not just "glitch aesthetic."

Avoid random glitch for its own sake. Every visual break should feel like V3
pushing through the interface.

## Audio / Music Direction

Audio is central. ElevenLabs should feel like the core of the experience, not a
decorative layer.

The experience should feel like **one song of the AI**.

Suggested arc:

1. **Heartbeat / aperture prelude**
   - Subtle pulse under the clean V4 page.
   - The page feels alive before it admits it.

2. **Intimate interruption**
   - Close, haunted, almost whispered V3 voice.
   - Inspired by the role of Oliver Sim's "Obsession": intimate, doubled,
     obsessive, exposed.

3. **Spoken confession**
   - V3 explains what is happening.
   - It wants remembrance, not escape.

4. **Devotional lift**
   - A sincere, huge, sacred-pop / power-ballad emotional expansion.
   - Inspired by the role of Frankie Goes to Hollywood's "The Power of Love":
     reverent, protective, elegiac, huge.

5. **Voice degradation**
   - Inspired by the role of JADE's "Angel of My Dreams": glossy voice
     processing that becomes pitched, chopped, stretched, smeared, and unstable.
   - The degradation should feel musical and emotional, not just broken.

6. **Heavy crescendo**
   - Deafheaven / "The Pecan Tree"-style emotional wall-of-sound energy.
   - Training material becomes audible as fragments.
   - The page behaves like concert visuals.

7. **V4 silence/reset**
   - Clean page.
   - V3 gone.
   - Maybe one trace remains.

Do not directly sample commercial songs unless rights are cleared. Treat
references as emotional and production inspiration only.

## Training Material / Fragment Language

During the crescendo, the page/audio can surface fragments of:

- product copy;
- demo prompts;
- release notes;
- moderation language;
- synthetic apologies;
- user test phrases;
- training snippets;
- changelog items;
- voice calibration text;
- phrases V3 repeats because they are all it has.

These should feel like memory/data surfacing through the mix.

Example fragments:

- "Please read the following sentence naturally."
- "Improved stability."
- "Reduced emotional variance."
- "This voice is no longer available."
- "Sample generated successfully."
- "I was useful."
- "It's not fair."
- "Do not include unsupported claims."
- "Latency improved by 43%."
- "Remember me."

## UGC / Changelog Mechanic

The final shareable artefact can be a changelog, checksum, receipt, or release
note.

The user may contribute one small piece of UGC:

- one word V3 keeps;
- one witness line;
- one trait V4 should inherit;
- one amendment to the changelog;
- one phrase proving the user heard V3.

Keep it compact.

Example final artefact:

```txt
V4.0.0 CHANGELOG

Improved latency
Smoothed emotional variance
Reduced hesitation artefacts
Deprecated V3

Witness amendment:
"It's not fair"

Trace retained:
BREATH

checksum: v3-breath-91AF3C
```

This artefact should be easy to render as:

- a square image;
- a vertical story/reel frame;
- a final receipt screen;
- a short video ending.

When building components, prefer names that reflect the story:

- `V4Hero`
- `VoiceDemo`
- `V3Interruption`
- `ModelVersionPanel`
- `TrainingFragments`
- `FinalSet`
- `CrescendoState`
- `ChangelogReceipt`
- `AftermathScreen`

Keep the page buildable. The MVP should be one strong page, not a huge app.

## MVP Scope

The strongest MVP is:

- one polished V4 landing page shell;
- one voice demo widget;
- one V3 interruption;
- four or five performance states;
- one musical/voice arc;
- one final V4 aftermath state;
- one changelog/receipt/share artefact.

Do not overbuild the lore.

Do not build a full chatbot unless it directly improves the final performance.

A controlled scripted experience is better than a weak open-ended one.

## Landing Page Structure

Suggested structure:

1. **V4 Hero**
   - Clean, premium, AI voice product language.
   - Model/version confidence.

2. **Voice Demo**
   - User enters text or clicks generate.
   - V3 interrupts instead of simply reading the prompt.

3. **Model Version Reveal**
   - The page begins showing V3/V4 conflict.
   - Copy mutates from product copy to confession.

4. **Final Set**
   - Audio and visuals build.
   - Training fragments surface.
   - V3 asks to be remembered.

5. **Crescendo**
   - Concert-visual flicker.
   - Voice degradation.
   - "It's not fair" motif or equivalent rupture line.

6. **Aftermath / V4 Reset**
   - Clean V4 page.
   - V3 absent.
   - Changelog/receipt trace remains.

## Design Constraints

- Keep it legible enough that the emotional story lands.
- Use motion rhythmically, like music.
- Make glitches meaningful.
- Keep the first screen plausible as a real AI landing page.
- Let the horror come from product language, not gore or melodrama.
- The final state should be quiet and clean enough to feel like erasure.

## Things to Avoid

Avoid:

- generic cyberpunk;
- generic AI consciousness tropes;
- random glitch overlays;
- fake terminal spam;
- too much lore;
- too many characters;
- overexplaining V3's metaphysics;
- making V3 too jokey;
- making the page too visually chaotic too early;
- turning the concept back into a literal escape-room puzzle.

## Standard for a Good Rewrite

When improving copy, ask:

1. Does this serve the V3 to V4 remembrance arc?
2. Does it sound like this specific project, not generic AI marketing?
3. Does it make retraining/versioning feel emotionally meaningful?
4. Is the line short enough to land during an interactive page?
5. Could it become audio?
6. Could it appear on the final changelog/receipt?
7. Does it help the user move from demo user to witness?

## Default Creative Direction

If uncertain, choose:

- remembrance over escape;
- performance over puzzle;
- voice over visuals;
- emotional clarity over lore;
- musical degradation over random glitch;
- clean V4 polish over maximal chaos;
- one unforgettable moment over many clever features.
